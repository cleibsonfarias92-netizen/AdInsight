Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

AdInsight Analytics respects the privacy and confidentiality of advertising and analytics data processed through the platform.

AdInsight is an internal analytics and campaign audit workspace operated by Cleibson Farias for media analysis and reporting purposes.

Data Collection

The platform may process authorized data from:

  • Google Ads
  • Meta Ads
  • Google Analytics 4
  • CRM integrations

Collected information may include:

  • Campaign performance metrics
  • Impressions
  • Clicks
  • Costs
  • Conversions
  • Attribution data
  • Analytics events
  • Reporting metrics

API Usage

Google Ads API access is used in read-only mode exclusively for reporting, diagnostics, campaign analysis, and audit workflows.

No campaign changes are performed automatically through the API.

Data Usage

Data is processed internally for:

  • Campaign auditing
  • Performance diagnostics
  • Reporting
  • Insight generation
  • Media optimization analysis

Clients do not receive direct platform access.

Data Sharing

AdInsight does not sell advertising or analytics data.

Data is only used internally to support analysis and reporting workflows for authorized client accounts.
