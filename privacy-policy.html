Privacy Policy

AdInsight is an internal paid media audit and analytics platform operated by Cleibson Farias to analyze managed client advertising accounts and prepare reports, diagnostics, and recommendations.

Data accessed through AdInsight is used solely for service delivery, analytical review, reporting, and performance diagnostics. Platform access is restricted to internal operational use and is not offered as a public client-facing application.

Advertising and analytics data may include campaign performance metrics, account structures, landing-page session signals, and CRM-linked lead context necessary for performance analysis. The Google Ads API is used in read-only mode for reporting and diagnostics.
