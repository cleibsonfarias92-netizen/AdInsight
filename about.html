About AdInsight

AdInsight is an internal marketing analytics and audit platform operated by Cleibson Farias.

The platform is used to support paid media analysis, campaign diagnostics, reporting workflows, and performance auditing for managed client advertising accounts.

AdInsight integrates data from Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics 4, and CRM systems to generate structured insights, identify inefficiencies, monitor campaign health, and assist in media optimization decisions.

The system is designed for internal operational use and is not offered as a public SaaS platform. Clients do not access the software directly. Reports and recommendations generated by the platform are delivered manually as part of consulting and media analysis services.

Main capabilities

  • Google Ads campaign performance analysis
  • Meta Ads diagnostics and monitoring
  • Funnel performance analysis
  • Cross-platform attribution diagnostics
  • Automated audit alerts
  • Internal reporting workflows
  • Campaign health scoring
  • Data normalization and analytics processing

AdInsight operates using read-only API access for authorized advertising accounts managed as part of media analysis and reporting services.
